Atelier & Bloom

A cozy floral party game · 1 florist in VR · up to 8 villagers on phones

🥽I'm the FloristOpen this on the VR headset (or a laptop to play with the mouse). 📱I'm a VillagerJoin from your phone with the 4-letter atelier code.
How a round works (≈1½ minutes)
  1. A villager walks in — one phone player is the customer and picks a heartfelt request: “I need something to say I'm sorry.”
  2. The florist arranges — grab stems from the buckets, trim with scissors, wrap in paper, tie a silk ribbon, or bend wire into a flower crown. Every flower has a meaning!
  3. Garden Sprites help… or meddle — the other phones cross-breed glowing hybrid flowers and send them to the florist (points if used!), or send a cheeky bee to steal a stem.
  4. Hand it over — the customer rates it with hearts and the secret mood is revealed.
